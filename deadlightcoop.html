Specialists survive together.

Move & shoot
WASD / arrows or touch joystick. Automatic fire and reload. Hold mouse to aim. T reloads early.

Plant & detonate
B plants at your feet. E detonates your own charges after a 0.5s arming delay. Squad and core take no blast damage.

Proximity mines · M
Tap MINE or press M to lay one at your feet for 25 shared salvage. It arms in 0.8s, triggers when an enemy comes close, and damages every enemy in its splash circle. Allies and the core are safe. Mines persist between waves; space them apart. Upgrade the harness for capacity, sensors for faster arming and trigger range, and Explosives for splash damage and radius.

Engineer
C builds barriers. G deploys K9s that patrol outward, hunt across the entire map, and cross allied barriers. Upgrade their weapons, batteries and pack size.

Medic
H heals nearby allies. V drops a 40s healing station. Stand near a fallen teammate to revive; triage upgrades speed this up.

Demolition
V plants a cluster charge. X triggers EMP to stun enemies. Choose C4, cryo or fire in the Workshop. E detonates all your armed charges.

Ranger
X activates Overdrive. Space dashes with brief protection. Specialist upgrades add piercing rounds and longer Overdrive.

Upgrade components
U opens the Workshop. Improve damage, firing rate, magazine, reload, armour, movement, explosives and your specialist equipment. Every fifth wave cleared unlocks higher ranks during the rebuilding break: six workshop stages through wave 30. All purchases carry forward. K9 pack capacity expands at stages 3 and 5.

Protect the core
R repairs nearby. Everyone shares salvage and core hull upgrades. Q pings for help. The host controls mission pace.

Zoom & phone view
Use + / −, pinch two fingers on the arena, or scroll the mouse wheel. Fit Map (0) shows the whole arena; Follow restores your view. Each device controls its own camera. Double taps do not zoom or scroll the page.

Supply relay · even waves
Stand in the gold ring for 12 seconds to capture it. More allies capture faster. Enemies in the ring block progress. Secure it before the 60s timer or wave ends for shared salvage, 30 Rally energy and a 35s core shield.

Squad Rally · F
Kills charge the squad meter. At 100%, any standing player can use Rally: heal 40 HP, revive and protect allies within 260 units for 3s. If the core is also nearby, it receives an 85-point shield for 25s.

Squad nuke · L
Choose 5-minute recharge, once per wave, or Off before solo play or creating a room. During a wave, press NUKE. Every connected teammate (including downed players) must confirm within 12s; solo needs one player. A 3s countdown follows, and anyone can cancel. The nuke destroys every enemy currently on the map, including bosses, and clears hostile projectiles. Reinforcements still arrive. Your squad and equipment are safe.

Nuke recharge
Five-minute mode recharges over 300 seconds of active play, across wave breaks; changing mission speed does not shorten it. Pausing freezes the clock. Once-per-wave mode unlocks at each new wave. A timeout or changed squad cancels the request without spending the nuke.

Enemy counters
Chargers lock a path before rushing: sidestep or interrupt with EMP. Kill Volatiles before their fuse ends. Focus Menders to stop enemy healing. Leave red artillery circles before impact. Bombs, mines and EMP strip blue enemy shields.

30 waves in six chapters. New threats unlock: Volatiles at wave 4, Chargers at 6, Menders at 8, and Mortars at 10. Every fifth wave brings a Breacher with artillery, projectile rings and shockwaves; below half health it attacks faster.

Every extra player adds more enemies, with larger additions in later waves. The squad size is locked at the start of each wave. The HUD shows its total enemy budget. Clear waves for salvage, healing and a 20-second rebuilding break.

Solo: an emergency respawn costs 30 core health. Co-op: stand near downed allies to revive them. The mission ends if every connected player is down.
