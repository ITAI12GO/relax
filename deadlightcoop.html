FIELD MANUAL

Your squad is your advantage.

Move and shoot
WASD / arrow keys or the touch joystick. Auto-fire targets nearby enemies. Hold the mouse to aim.

Share every pickup
Walk over salvage and supply crates. It all goes into the squad's shared wallet.

Revive a teammate
Stand close to a downed ally for 3 seconds. A Medic needs only 1.8 seconds. Reviving costs nothing.

Use your role
Ranger fires faster. Medic heals more. Engineer builds cheaper and keeps the robot dog longer.

Fortify and repair
B places a barrier in your movement direction. R repairs the core when you are close enough.

Call support
H heals nearby allies. G calls a robot dog. U upgrades your weapon. Q marks your position for help.

All purchases use shared salvage. A dog lasts 22 seconds (Engineer: 30). Twelve waves include a heavy boss every fourth wave. Clear a wave to earn salvage and a short rebuilding break.

Solo: emergency respawn costs 30 core health. Online: teammates revive you; the mission ends if the whole active squad goes down. Only the host can pause or start the next wave early.
