FIELD MANUAL · 30 WAVES

Specialists survive together.

Move & shoot
WASD / arrows or touch joystick. Automatic fire and reload. Hold mouse to aim. T reloads early.

Plant & detonate
B plants at your feet. E detonates your own charges after a 0.5s arming delay. Squad and core take no blast damage.

Engineer
C builds barriers. G deploys K9s that patrol outward, hunt across the entire map, and cross allied barriers. Upgrade their weapons, batteries and pack size.

Medic
H heals nearby allies. V drops a 40s healing station. Stand near a fallen teammate to revive; triage upgrades speed this up.

Demolition
V plants a cluster charge. X triggers EMP to stun enemies. Choose C4, cryo or fire in the Workshop. E detonates all your armed charges.

Ranger
X activates Overdrive. Space dashes with brief protection. Specialist upgrades add piercing rounds and longer Overdrive.

Upgrade components
U opens the Workshop. Improve damage, firing rate, magazine, reload, armour, movement, explosives and your specialist equipment.

Protect the core
R repairs nearby. Everyone shares salvage and core hull upgrades. Q pings for help. The host controls mission pace.

30 waves in six chapters. Runner rushes, armoured sieges and a boss every fifth wave. Clear waves to earn salvage, healing and a 20-second rebuilding break.

Solo: an emergency respawn costs 30 core health. Co-op: stand near downed allies to revive them. The mission ends if every connected player is down.
