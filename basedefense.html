Unit intel

Eight infantry roles and three xeno strains. Reinforcements rotate through a shuffled bag holding one of every role, so each side ends up with an even spread rather than a lucky streak of snipers.

RoleHPArmourDamageRangeRateSpeedvs BaseValue

Armour is flat damage reduction on every hit (minimum 1 gets through). Rate is seconds between shots. vs Base is the damage multiplier against command posts. Value is the points awarded to whoever kills it — xeno kills pay a bonus on top.

Target selection. A soldier scans roughly 540 units around itself and picks a target at random from everything it can see. Closer targets, wounded targets, medics and flag carriers come up more often, but nothing is guaranteed — fire spreads across a firefight instead of every rifle locking onto the same neighbour. Targets are held for a couple of seconds before the next roll, and about four in ten troops deploy with assault orders that pull them toward an enemy command post instead.
