How to play & roster notes

Shoot: choose a target in the goal or use the six target buttons. Set your power, start the timing meter, then kick when the white marker reaches the green area. More power is harder to control. Aim near the posts for greater risk and reward.

Save: read the opponent’s hint, select one of six areas, and commit your dive. Directions are always from your screen’s viewpoint. Hints are imperfect. Keyboard: 1–6 selects a target, Space starts/stops the meter or dives, P pauses.

Change teams: use either country dropdown before playing. Swap sides exchanges both countries and their selected players. During a match, press Switch teams at the top, then confirm. You return to team selection; the current shootout and Cup run end. The next match starts at 0–0.

Each team gets up to five kicks, ending early when the other side cannot catch up. A tie goes to sudden death, with equal turns before deciding the winner. This arcade format lets your chosen star take every kick. Cup run is a simplified five-round knockout challenge using the 2026 countries, not a recreation of the full group stage or official bracket. Your first opponent is your selection; four new countries follow if you keep winning.

All 48 countries and two selected players per country are drawn from FIFA’s 2026 World Cup squad lists (19 July 2026 edition; checked 9 September 2026).
