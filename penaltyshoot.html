Player stats are fictional arcade ratings out of 100, created for this game; they are not official FIFA or EA ratings or real career statistics. Higher accuracy reduces shot spread, power makes shots harder to save, and composure widens the timing sweet spot.

How to play & roster notes

Shoot: choose a target in the goal or use the six target buttons. Set your power, start the timing meter, then kick when the white marker reaches the green area. More power is harder to control. Aim near the posts for greater risk and reward.

Save: read the opponent’s hint, select one of six areas, and commit your dive. Directions are always from your screen’s viewpoint. Hints are imperfect. Keyboard: 1–6 selects a target, Space starts/stops the meter or dives, P pauses.

Each team gets up to five kicks, ending early when the other side cannot catch up. A tie goes to sudden death, with equal turns before deciding the winner. This arcade format lets your chosen star take every kick. In Cup run, your first opponent is your selection; four new countries follow if you keep winning.

Uses countries and selected players from the 2022 FIFA World Cup squads, not current squads. Independent fan game. No affiliation with FIFA. Everything needed to play is contained in this file; the roster reference link is optional.
